Stay in touch and share everyday care

Make room for everyone in the conversation, including the person receiving care. Share everyday messages, respond to each other and keep reminders in the same family space.

What changes day to day

Check-ins reach all of you

Each “Good morning” or “I’m so-so” is shared with the family. With notifications enabled, the phone can play a different sound for each message type. The history shows how many have read it; this does not confirm that someone has acted on it.

Keep medication reminders together

You add each medicine by scanning the box, set its times and choose who the app alerts for each dose. The person marks it as taken.

Medical appointments live in one place

Date, place and notes for each appointment, with a reminder. No more asking the group when the cardiologist was.

You can reply without typing

As well as typing or dictating, you have quick messages to reply with one tap: “How are you?”, “Do you need anything?”, “Take care”.

What the family decides

The buttons
Which buttons the older person sees, in what order, and which ones you add with your own wording.
Who joins
The administrator generates invite codes, assigns each person’s role and can remove members.
The history
You can export all the family’s conversations as PDF, CSV or text — handy to take to an appointment.
The cost
A single subscription, the administrator’s, covers the whole family with no limit on members.

Why sharing information matters

Risk factors for caregiver burden include the number of hours spent caring and social isolation6. Having every relative see the same thing does not reduce the work of caring, but it makes it easier to share.

See the evidence and its limits

What DiAlgo is not

It is not telecare or an emergency service: “I need help” alerts the family, not the emergency services. Nor does it monitor or track anyone. It only shows what the person chooses to send.

Start with your family

The first person to install it creates the family and invites the rest.
