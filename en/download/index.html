Download DiAlgo

For iPhone and Android. Install it on your own phone first: from there you create the family and invite the others.

Before you install

Price
30-day free trial, no card. Then €9.99 a month in Spain for the whole family.
Who pays
Only the person who manages the family. Everyone else installs it and joins with an invite code.
What you need
An iPhone or Android phone with an internet connection for each person who will use it.
Languages
English, Spanish and Catalan. Choose your language in the app’s profile settings.

And then?

Creating the family, inviting the others and setting up buttons, medication and visits takes a few minutes. We walk you through it step by step.
