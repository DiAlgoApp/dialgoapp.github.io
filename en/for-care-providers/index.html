Bring people and their caregivers into the conversation

For home care services, day centres and organisations supporting families. We can build on DiAlgo to make everyday communication simpler, with two ways to collaborate.

Two ways to work together

DiAlgo with your branding

Offer DiAlgo to the families you support, with your name and logo inside the app. Give your service a presence in their everyday communication space.

Visual branding keeps the DiAlgo experience. We agree the scope, support and collaboration terms in writing.

An app adapted to your own service

We build on how DiAlgo works to develop an app with your identity and the adaptations you need: screens, messages or communication workflows.

We define the scope, distribution and maintenance together before development.

The communication we want to make easier

The person takes part

Simple buttons and messages help them share how they feel or ask someone to get in touch.

Family can respond

Updates and replies share the same space, alongside medication and appointments added by the family.

Caregivers join by invitation

Professionals take part in a family group with the appropriate authorisation and permissions.

How we work with you

Your service
Tell us by message or email who you support and which conversations you want to make easier.
A written proposal
We respond with a proposed approach and adaptations. Together we agree the terms, responsibilities and support for your service.
Getting started
We agree the setup, family guidance and steps to put it into practice.

Branding and access permissions are separate

Showing your name or logo does not give your organisation access to conversations, medication or appointments. Families control who joins their group. Visual branding does not change their subscription.

Read about security and privacy

Where DiAlgo fits in care

DiAlgo supports everyday communication. It is not a telecare or emergency service, and it does not replace your care management software.

Tell us what you want to make easier

Write to us about your service and which approach interests you. We will continue the conversation by message or email.
