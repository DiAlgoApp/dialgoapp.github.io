What we store, where, and who can see it

DiAlgo holds family messages, medication and medical appointments: this is health data. This page explains plainly how it is handled, including what the app does not do.

The data

What is stored
Your name and email, the families you belong to and your role in them, messages, medication with its schedule, medical appointments and the identifier that allows notifications to reach your phone.
Where
Messages, medication and visits are stored in a Google Firebase database hosted in the European Union (region europe-west1, Belgium). DiAlgo’s server code runs in that same region.
Third-party services
Sign-in and notifications use Google (Firebase) and Apple. Subscriptions are billed by the App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat managing their status. Firebase also collects crash diagnostics and technical metrics. These providers may process data outside the European Union under their terms.
Encryption
All communication between the app and the server is encrypted in transit (TLS), and Google encrypts stored data. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Access

Who sees a family’s data
Only its members. The database rules reject any read or write from someone who does not belong to that family.
How people join a family
With an invite code generated by the administrator. Ten failed attempts temporarily limit further code attempts within a one-hour window.
Roles
Administrator, relative and older person have different permissions. Only the administrator manages members, buttons, medication and visits.
Passwords and payments
DiAlgo does not store your password: sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, or directly by Google or Apple. Nor do we see your card; payment is processed by the store.

Your rights

Export
From the app you can export your family’s conversation history as PDF, CSV or text.
Delete your account
You do this from your profile, inside the app. If you pay the subscription, cancel it first in the App Store or Google Play: deleting the account does not stop the charge.
Access, rectification and erasure
You can exercise your rights over your personal data by writing to dialgo@onmedic.org.
